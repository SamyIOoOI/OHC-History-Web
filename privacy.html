This policy explains how information is handled when you use The History Web at thehistoryweb.org. You do not need an account to read the site.

1. Information you choose to share

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach.

The article-submission form asks for your name, email address, article title and a link to your article. It also lets you provide social-media contact details, choose whether we may contact you there, and leave comments. The form is hosted by Google Forms. Google handles information under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

We use information you send to review submissions, reply to you and manage the contribution. Please do not send passwords, payment details or other sensitive information. If you want to ask about information you have submitted, contact samy.historyweb@gmail.com.

2. Settings stored in your browser

The site uses local storage to remember settings such as your theme, font, title colour, cursor, CRT effect and floating characters. These settings stay in your browser; the settings code does not send them to us. It also uses session storage to remember a page-loading state during your browsing session.

You can remove this information through your browser's site-data settings. Clearing it will reset your saved choices. These settings are separate from advertising cookies and advertising-consent choices.

3. Hosting, fonts and visitor totals

The site is delivered through Cloudflare. Delivering and protecting the site involves processing technical information such as your IP address, requested pages and browser information. We use aggregate traffic statistics to understand visits and pageviews. Cloudflare's privacy policy is available at https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/.

The public visitor counter displays a stored aggregate total. The counter code reads that total from a file; it does not ask for your name or email or create a visitor account.

Some fonts load from Google Fonts. When your browser requests a font, Google receives technical information such as your IP address and request details. See https://developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy.

4. Advertising and cookies

When Google AdSense advertising is enabled on the site, Google and other third-party advertising vendors may use cookies, device identifiers or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, limit repeated ads and help prevent fraud.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website or other websites. Google's advertising cookies allow Google and its partners to serve personalized ads based on visits to this site and other sites on the internet, where permitted and subject to your choices.

Non-personalized ads may still use cookies or similar technologies for purposes such as measurement, frequency limits and fraud prevention. Turning off personalized advertising does not necessarily remove ads or all advertising-related data processing.

Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

5. Your advertising choices

You can manage Google's personalized advertising settings at https://myadcenter.google.com/.

You can opt out of interest-based advertising from participating third-party vendors at https://www.aboutads.info/choices/. Choices may apply only to the browser or device you are using and may need to be set again after you clear cookies.

You can also block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Some features or saved preferences may stop working as expected.

For visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, advertising will be subject to the choices collected through the site's consent message where consent is required. You will be able to accept or reject optional purposes and change or withdraw your choice through "Privacy choices" in the footer. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that lawfully occurred before withdrawal.

6. Other websites

History articles link to outside sources. Following a link takes you to another website, whose privacy practices may differ from ours. Review that website's policy before sharing personal information there.

7. Your information and this policy

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, object to certain uses, or withdraw consent. Email samy.historyweb@gmail.com with a request or a privacy question. We may need enough information to verify your request. Requests about information handled independently by Google, Cloudflare or another provider may need to be made to that provider.

We may update this policy as the site or its services change. The date above shows the latest revision.

Contact: samy.historyweb@gmail.com
