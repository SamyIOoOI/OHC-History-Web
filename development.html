Coverage through 1924 5 November 2026

  • Added the Nandi Resistance with a cropped period painting by Harry Johnston (1902), verified reading sources, and updated navigation, downloads and search.
  • Added The Whiskey Rebellion and Federal Authority, 1791-1794, with a period painting and three reading sources, and restored The Great Migration, 1916-1924, with an 1890 period engraving of an earlier exodus from North Carolina.
  • Added The Pequot War, Archaic North America and the Shift to Settled Communities, and Potosi, Silver Mining and the Colonial Mita, each with a period illustration, a verified reading list and updated navigation and search.
  • Added The Powhatan Confederacy and English Colonization with an 1880 engraving, a two-source reading list and updated navigation and search.
  • Historical coverage now ends in 1924. Later events have been removed or replaced; source-publication dates are separate from the historical timeline.
  • Expanded Americas coverage with new topics, period artwork, and linked reading sources.
  • Updated the topic catalog, era lists, search, sitemap, and downloadable pages. Pre and Colonial navigation is unchanged.
  • Reviewed STEM illustrations and source links.
