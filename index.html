> How to use this website?

At the bottom of the page hover on a continent and click it to view an overview of its history divided by time periods & reading sources. Remember to always take what you read with caution. Not all information is accurate or unbiased.

> Where do I submit an article?

To submit an article, you must be part of the October Humanities Club or have prior approval. Contact us here for more information.

> What is The October Humanities Club?

OHC or October Humanities Club is a student-led organization focused on humanities, philosophy, debate and history with a friendly community & it is one of the few Humanities clubs in all of Egypt.
